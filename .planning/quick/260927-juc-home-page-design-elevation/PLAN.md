# Home page design elevation (trial)

Make the hand-drawn cat tree read as a chosen illustration rather than a kids'
drawing, without changing Heidi's linework. Built as an uncommitted trial; Heidi
reviews and drops items before anything is committed.

1. Outline recoloured black → cocoa `#4A3527` (chosen from charcoal / espresso / cocoa)
2. Muted fills — skipped, undecided
3. Paper grain over the drawing
4. Editorial hero: logo + headline + copy beside the tree, white card removed
5. Contact shadow under the tree's base
6. Callout labels with leader lines (md and up); pills on perches below md
7. Hovering a label lights up its perch
8. Hanging toys sway gently (off under reduced motion)

All pixel work happens at load time from `public/images/cat-tree.png`
(`lib/catTreeArt.ts`); no new image files.
